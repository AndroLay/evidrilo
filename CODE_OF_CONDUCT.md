# Evidrilo Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Evidrilo welcomes good-faith participation in building a respectful, safe, and
constructive project. Everyone should be treated with dignity, regardless of
age, disability or neurodiversity, appearance, race, ethnicity, caste, sex,
gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, nationality, language,
religion or philosophy, education, experience, or economic or social position.

## Expected conduct

- Be considerate and respectful, including when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than attacking people.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make a reasonable effort to repair harm.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and consent.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment; discriminatory or demeaning language; personal attacks or threats;
unwanted sexual attention; publishing someone's private information without
permission; impersonation or evading a moderation action; and deliberate
disruption or repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. This also includes
encouraging or threatening violence or other harm. Context matters: a
good-faith disagreement or an honest mistake is not, by itself, a violation.

## Where this applies

This policy applies to project spaces maintained by Evidrilo, including this
repository's issues, pull requests, code reviews, and discussions, and to
people representing Evidrilo at an event or through an official project
channel.

## Reporting and response

Report a concern privately to the maintainer at
[andrlay30@gmail.com](mailto:andrlay30@gmail.com). Include links or other
relevant context, but do not publish incident details in a public issue or pull
request. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practicable. The maintainer
will limit access to report details and preserve confidentiality to the extent
reasonably possible while reviewing and responding.

The project currently has a single maintainer and no independent internal
appeal channel. If a report concerns the maintainer or the contact address is
unavailable, use GitHub's reporting controls for the relevant content or
account rather than posting sensitive details publicly.

Depending on the circumstances and severity, the maintainer may clarify
expectations, issue a warning, edit or remove content, reject a contribution,
restrict participation, or block a person temporarily or permanently from
project spaces. Serious incidents may warrant stronger action without earlier
steps. Enforcement should be proportionate and handled privately where
practicable.
